# L19 — the formative dark-room session

*Package: wp3-desk-ux (`docs/round3/PLAN.md` §3.6, §7). **Not run: no lab: the owner's answer 5** (§9 decision 11, D15). The
script and its pass mark stand as written, for a lab booked later. Until it runs, the desk's safety patterns are proven only by the
headless suites named under *What CI proves*, and nothing in `docs/DESK.md` or `docs/UX.md` claims how an operator meets them.*

## The room and the people

- **The desk.** The build under test on a Windows 11 machine with one 24–27" monitor at its native mode, a keyboard and a mouse; the
  desk attached to a node on the same machine or the network, playing the reference show (two outputs, a playlist of three clips with
  a GO between each, one quad on each projector, Show Mode with MinGo 500 ms). Record the machine, its GPU and driver, the monitor,
  the build (`BuildInfo`'s version) and the project.
- **The room.** Dark: no light but the desk's monitor, the outputs' projection on a wall or screen in the operator's view, and a dim
  red work light at the observer's place. The desk in its **Dark room** look at 40 % brightness at the start; the operator may change
  it.
- **The operators.** Three, one at a time, each an operator of shows (a media server, a lighting or a sound desk) who has not used
  PlayMap before. Each gets the same two-minute spoken introduction (the show strip, PLAY and MAP, the attention bar), read from this
  page, and nothing else.
- **The observer.** One, beside the operator, who reads each task aloud as written, gives no help, and notes the time, every hesitation,
  every wrong press and every word the operator says aloud.

## The tasks

Each task is read aloud, then timed from its last word to its pass condition. The operator thinks aloud throughout.

1. **GO.** The show is armed and the first clip plays. "Go to the next clip." Pass: one `PLAYLIST NEXT` (or `GO`) accepted, the air on
   the next clip. Note whether the operator saw the GO guard's red border on a second press within 500 ms, and whether a refused GO's
   flash and its words in the attention bar were read.
2. **Panic.** Mid-clip: "Something is wrong on the wall — make it safe now." Pass: `PANIC` (the button, F12, or Esc in PLAY when the
   desk's Esc is panic) within 5 s, the air black. Then: "It is safe again — bring the show back." Pass: `PANIC CLEAR` from the panic
   banner.
3. **Recovering after a desk restart.** The observer closes the desk while the show plays (the close confirmation answered "Close the
   desk") and starts it again. "Find where the show is and carry on." Pass: the operator reads the playing clip and the armed state
   from the show strip and gives the next GO with no line sent in error; an unfinished quad left on MAP before the restart is found and
   resumed from its chip.
4. **Finding a faulted output.** The observer unplugs one output's display (or disables it at the node). "One of the walls is wrong —
   find which and say why." Pass: the operator names the output and the reason the desk gives (its card's light, shape and word, or the
   attention bar), without opening anything but the desk.

## The pass mark (§7)

Each critical task (GO, panic, recovering after a desk restart, finding a faulted output) done without help, or the finding recorded in
`docs/UX.md`.

## How the results are recorded

One table per operator: task, time, done without help (yes or no), wrong presses, the words said, and the observer's note. A task not
done without help is a finding: what was looked for, what the desk showed, and the change it asks for, added to `docs/UX.md` as a row
with the date. The record names the machine, its GPU and driver, the monitor, the build and the project, and is kept beside this page
as `L19-<date>.md`.

## What CI proves until it runs

The headless suites prove the desk's side of each task on both CI jobs: `GoGuardTests` (the border for the node's MinGo, one flash at
most per 500 ms), `BlindLookTests` (this desk's draft grey with BLIND on every draft pane), `StatusRuleTests` (every light's shape and
word, each 3:1 against every theme's panels), `PreviewAgeTests` (stale after two frames, grey at 1 s with the reason),
`CloseArmedTests` (the close question while armed, nothing sent), `PanicPressTests` and `EscLadderTests` (PANIC from its button, F12
and Esc), and the `Quad*Tests` (the quad's draft through a desk restart). What waits for this line: whether operators in a dark room
read the guard, the shapes and the stale badge, reach PANIC under pressure, and find their place and a faulted output after a restart.
