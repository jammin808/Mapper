# The desk

The desk (`src/PlayMap.App`) keeps three workspaces (PLAY, MAP, SCAN) and adds a show strip, an attention bar, four
drawers and generic parts, so most round-2 features add data, not panels. It sends verbs and reads STATE: every button,
right-click entry and key names the catalogue line it sends, the same line the wire, MCP and Companion send. A line whose
catalogue row presses hold, as a Companion key holds it, is held to confirm wherever the desk sends it from a button or a menu — the
desk's own controls, a card's line, a generated entry (FAILOVER, a pattern, an output closed or identified, a surface hidden or
given a source, a blind commit, a re-solve, LIGHT WALL, PAIRING NEW among them): its button sends only when held to the end of its
ring, and in a menu it is pressed twice — the first press arms the entry, which then reads "Press again to send <line>" and sends
nothing; a second press of it within 3 s of the desk's clock sends that line once (a line a push moved in between arms the entry
afresh, naming the new line); another entry, the menu closing or made again, or the 3 s passing lets it go. Three such rows the
desk sends at every press wherever they are, with the air's controls: the transport's Stop (`VIDEO STOP`), the panic banner's Clear
(`PANIC CLEAR`) and the master (`MASTER`); PANIC, Blackout, GO, NEXT, PREV, Play and Pause are held nowhere. An inspector's field
and a drag send their line as they change. Menu entries show their words as they are (underscores and all; a menu has no access
keys).

not sent and a toast gives the node's words.

## Wire-only in round 2

These run over the wire, HTTP, MCP, Companion and the bindings; on the desk they are a generic STATE card and the generated
right-click entries, with no view of their own: the effects browser and inspector, modulation sockets, tempo, bindings and learn,
timecode, LED fixture placement and patching, optimise jobs, live inputs and sends, the phone scan path, align, the facade guide,
the sync and framelock tab, the present path chips (`PRESENT PATH`), the per-output panic plans (`PANIC SET`), Companion decks, the
content library. Every package's card renders from its final fixture, and every
card action sends its catalogue line (`EveryCardTests`).

## The spine other desk packages build on

`DeskModule` (slots, banners, shortcuts, menu extras, badges, inspector sections, drawer tabs, the gate, an Esc rung),
composed by one partial hook per package (`MainViewModel.Spine|Show|Content|Map.cs`), each push taken in two passes (every module
takes it, the gate's facts move, then every module re-reads its actions, so none asks the gate with the push before); an action
raises only what moved; views registered with `ModuleViews`;
`StateSnapshot.Root` and `Section<T>` (read once per snapshot); `IMenuTarget` on every row and inspector; `DeskSession.Gate`
and `DeskSession.Verbs`; `StatusRow`, `HoldButton`, `ScoreRing`, `DeskDrop`; the param templates in
`Theme/ParamTemplates.axaml` with a socket slot; `Theme/Desk1|2|3.axaml`. `DeskVerbRuleTests` walks every reachable action
with every fixture loaded and fails on a line the catalogue does not parse, a line with a secret, or a toolbar not in its
menu.

## The top bar

The bar is one row when everything fits and the window is at least 1366 px; otherwise two rows (identity, workspaces and
drawers; then transport, timecode, tempo and the show cluster). With the full show cluster one row wants about 1966 px,
so a 1920 px window shows two rows. Below 1280 px the transport drops its position column and the drawer pills are icons;
below 1200 px Simple|Pro leaves the bar (Ctrl+Shift+P still switches). No child ends beyond the window at 1100, 1280,
1366, 1600 or 1920 px; the show cluster never shrinks. The mode frame takes no room, so arming changes nothing in the bar.
